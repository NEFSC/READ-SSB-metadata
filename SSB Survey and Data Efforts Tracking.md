
*SSB Metrics Catalog*
Link: https://nefsc.github.io/READ-SSB-SSB_METRICS_CATALOG/index.html

Purpose: A comprehensive catalog of all metrics produced by SSB that are currently or indented to be used by others within the NEFSC, council staff, academic partners or other stakeholders. This can help end users identify which metrics SSB produces, their level of observation and compare and contrast across metrics produced to facilitate selecting socioeconomic metrics in end products. This effort aims to decrease redundancy and improve clarity on SSB produced metrics and the methods used to develop them.

*Project: SSB Inventory Sheet*
Purpose: This inventory sheet is used to track all primary data colelctions, seccondary data and outward facing products produced by the SSB as a part of the efforts of the SSB Data Working Group (established 12/2025. 

Goal: Increase transparency of all economic and socioeconomic efforts which the SSB is leading or has staff involved in and increase knowledge/ accessibility to the data sources and data summaries which are most likely not captured in other metadata sources (such as the data dictionary).
Contributing: Any member of the SSB is encouraged to update this sheet as survey or data efforts occur or as increased information is published around each effort.
